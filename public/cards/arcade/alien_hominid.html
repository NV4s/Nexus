Alien Hominid

Alien Hominid

Developer: The Behemoth

Release Date: August 7, 2002

Overview

Alien Hominid is a side-scrolling Run and Gun Shooter in a similar vein to games such as Metal Slug, where one hit instantly kills and has a two-player simultaneous play.


Gameplay

Players take over as the titular hominid, who has to fend off waves of secret agents. His main arsenal is a blaster, while players can also melee close-up enemies and use a limited number of grenades to attack.

Advanced moves include:

  • Rolling under shots
  • Jumping on and biting off enemies' heads
  • Temporarily scaring other enemies
  • Digging underground to drag enemies down with them

Features

Players can collect a numerous variety of power-ups which simultaneously give players extra grenades, a shield, and unique ammo. Players can also drive vehicles, ride on top of a Yeti, and pilot a UFO.

Completing certain tasks will unlock hats the players can dress their Hominid in. The main game features sixteen stages.
